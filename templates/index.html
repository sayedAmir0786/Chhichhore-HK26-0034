      About EcoShield AI

      AI-Powered Environmental Protection and Monitoring System (SD-01)

      What it does

      EcoShield AI helps users compare forest images, explore observed fire hotspots, and check environmental claims against trusted references, all in one dashboard.

      Technology

      HTML, CSS and JavaScript frontend; Python Flask backend; SQLite storage; OpenCV and Pillow for images; Chart.js and Leaflet for charts and maps.

      Sample data vs live data

      • Fire hotspots: sample demonstration data unless a NASA FIRMS key is configured.
      • Starter records: forest and claim records marked "Sample" are demonstration data.
      • Your uploads and claim checks: real records created by you.

      Limitations

      • Pixel comparison is not validated forest classification and cannot prove illegal logging.
      • Hotspot observations do not predict future fires.
      • The claim checker is keyword matching against 12 reference entries, not a trained AI model.
